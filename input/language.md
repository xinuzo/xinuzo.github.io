## {{ hl("language", "Language Learning") }}

Outside of mathematics, one of my favourite ways to spend time is learning languages.
I'm currently studying both **Japanese** and **Chinese (Mandarin)** — for quite
different reasons, but both equally rewarding.

### Japanese 🇯🇵

I started learning Japanese because I plan to **study abroad in Japan**. Being able
to navigate daily life, read academic materials, and connect with people in their
own language feels essential to making the most of that experience. Japanese grammar
is fascinating in its own right — the way particles work, the layers of politeness,
and the interplay of three writing systems (hiragana, katakana, kanji) make it a
genuinely engaging puzzle.

### Chinese (Mandarin) 🇨🇳

Chinese is purely a **hobby** for me. I was drawn to it by curiosity — the tonal
system, the character-based writing, and the sheer depth of cultural and literary
heritage. There's something deeply satisfying about slowly being able to read texts
that have been around for thousands of years. Plus, as an Indonesian of Chinese
descent, it feels like reconnecting with part of my heritage.

### Recommended Resources

Here are the apps and channels I use regularly and genuinely recommend:

#### Apps

- **[Yomu Yomu](https://yomuyomu.app/)** — A wonderful Japanese reading app with
  graded stories. Great for building reading fluency at any level. I use it almost
  daily.

- **[Du Chinese](https://www.duchinese.net/)** — The Chinese equivalent of Yomu Yomu.
  Graded reading with pinyin support, audio, and vocabulary tracking. Excellent for
  building comprehension progressively.

#### YouTube Channels

**Japanese:**
- [Japanese Ammo with Misa](https://www.youtube.com/@JapaneseAmmowithMisa) — One of the
  most popular free Japanese grammar channels. Misa explains concepts clearly with great
  examples.
- [Nihongo no Mori](https://www.youtube.com/@nihaboradio) — Comprehensive JLPT preparation
  content, all in Japanese (great for immersion).
- [Comprehensible Japanese](https://www.youtube.com/@ComprehensibleJapanese) — Slow,
  clear Japanese with visual aids — perfect for beginners.

**Chinese:**
- [Mandarin Corner](https://www.youtube.com/@MandarinCorner) — Real-life conversations
  and street interviews with Chinese/pinyin subtitles.
- [Chinese with Shuo](https://www.youtube.com/@ChinesewithShuo) — Engaging lessons that
  cover grammar, vocabulary, and cultural context.
- [Everyday Chinese](https://www.youtube.com/@EverydayChinese888) — Bite-sized lessons
  for practical, everyday Mandarin.
