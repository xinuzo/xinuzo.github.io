## {{ hl("about", "About") }}

I am an undergraduate studying mathematics at ITB, with a particular
focus on combinatorics, algebra, and their intersections.
As a high school student I was a finalist at the
National Science Olympiad (OSN) in Mathematics.
At university I received a Silver Medal at KOALA
(Kompetisi Aljabar Mahasiswa) and at the
Mathematics Analysis and Geometry Day.

I served as a Section Leader for
[Code in Place](https://codeinplace.stanford.edu/)
at Stanford University, tutored students for UTBK exams,
and am active in [HIMATIKA ITB](https://himatika.org/).

Outside of math, I develop software —
from mobile games to browser tools —
and create visual math content using
[Manim](https://www.manim.community/) and Canva.

You can browse my [math notes](math-notes.html),
[software projects](software.html),
or [teaching materials](teaching.html).
