Below are expository notes on topics I have studied
or am currently exploring. Each document is a
self-contained PDF intended to be accessible to undergraduates.

## {{ hl("combinatorics", "Combinatorics") }}

- [Enumerative Combinatorics: Generating Functions and Bijective Proofs](#)
  `(PDF, 18 pp.)`
  — An introduction to ordinary and exponential generating
  functions, covering the Catalan numbers, Stirling numbers,
  and the transfer matrix method.

- [Extremal Combinatorics and the Kruskal-Katona Theorem](#)
  `(PDF, 12 pp.)`
  — Shadow operators on simplicial complexes and
  $f$-vector inequalities.

## {{ hl("matroid-theory", "Matroid Theory") }}

- [Introduction to Matroids: From Graphs to Geometry](#)
  `(PDF, 24 pp.)`
  — Cryptomorphic axiom systems (independent sets, bases,
  circuits, rank, closure, flats) with motivating examples
  from graph theory and linear algebra.

- [Matroid Intersection and Applications](#)
  `(PDF, 10 pp.)`
  — Edmonds' matroid intersection theorem and
  its applications to bipartite matching, arborescences,
  and rainbow-free colourings.

## {{ hl("lorentzian", "Lorentzian Polynomials") }}

- [Lorentzian Polynomials and Log-Concavity](#)
  `(PDF, 30 pp.)`
  — Following Brändén–Huh, we develop the theory of
  Lorentzian polynomials and prove that the sequence
  $\{W_k(M)\}$ of Whitney numbers of the second kind
  of a matroid $M$ is log-concave.

- [Notes on the Hodge–Riemann Relations for Matroids](#)
  `(PDF, 15 pp.)`
  — Sketch of the Adiprasito–Huh–Katz proof of
  the Heron–Rota–Welsh conjecture via the Kähler package
  for the Chow ring $A^\bullet(M)$.
